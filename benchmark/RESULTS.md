split for tabular datasets, the last H dates for time series.
`cv` is what each system reported before seeing that data — the gap
between `cv` and `holdout` is the number worth looking at.

`=base` in the leaky column means the dataset has no categorical
columns, so the leaky variant is identical to the baseline.
For log loss and RMSE lower is better; for AUC and accuracy higher is.

## Headline

Fast preset against a single default LightGBM, on the held-out split:
**12 wins, 2 losses, 1 tie** across 15 datasets.
Not a win on: breast-cancer (loss), wine-multiclass (loss), air-passengers (tie).

Worst case for the leaky shortcut, on **telco-churn**: it reports a cross-validated auc of 0.9987 and delivers 0.7247 on unseen rows (gap 0.2740, 38% optimistic). The same split through this pipeline: 0.8468 reported, 0.8428 delivered (gap 0.0040).
